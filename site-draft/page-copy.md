## radio.audiobookmanager.com (the landing page)

**ABM Radio**

Free internet radio from AudioBook Manager. No ads, no accounts, no tracking: press play and leave
it running while you sort your library, read, or work.

Every track on air is openly licensed, and each one is credited, with a link back to the artist.

**Stations**

- **ABM Lofi:** warm, jazzy lofi beats for long sessions. *Listen →*
- **Story Time** *(coming later)*: public-domain stories read aloud, between the music.

## radio.audiobookmanager.com/lofi

**ABM Lofi**

Lofi beats for listening while you sort your audiobooks.

*(the player goes here; see `player-spec.md`)*

**About this station**

ABM Lofi plays music its artists have released under open licences, mostly Creative Commons
Attribution, and nothing else. I check each track's licence when I add it, save a copy of the page
that grants it, and keep the whole catalog public in the
[abm-radio repo](https://github.com/sunbrolynk/abm-radio). The [credits page](/credits) lists
every track that can play and who made it.

**Listen anywhere**

The player above works in any browser. To use your own app (VLC, a phone radio app, a smart
speaker), add one of these:

- `https://stream.audiobookmanager.com/listen/lofi/radio.aac`: best sound, works almost
  everywhere
- `https://stream.audiobookmanager.com/listen/lofi/radio.mp3`: for older players

**Artists**

If you're an artist and want a track taken off the air, email
[admin@audiobookmanager.com](mailto:admin@audiobookmanager.com) and I'll remove it. If you'd like
your music played here, the same address works.

**Privacy**

This page uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. The streaming server sees your IP
address while you listen, like any website does. It keeps only listener totals, not who listened,
and its logs are cleared after about a week.
